# Standard Operating Procedure: New Project Spin-Up (Smartsheet)

**Source:** Six screen-recording video transcripts of the current process
**Purpose:** Set up everything a new project needs in Smartsheet and OneDrive, from the alert email to the final archive.
**Format note:** Video and timestamp references like (Video 2, 1:03) point back to the recordings so you can check any step against the source footage.

---

## Before You Start: What You Need

1. Access to the Smartsheet account where the alert emails arrive.
2. Editor access to the Gen 009 checklist template, the Master Project List, the Orders sheet, and each project's artifacts folder.
3. A second browser profile called **Smartsheet Admin** with the Dynamic View login saved in it. This is required for Tasks 9 and 10. (Video 5, 1:42)
4. Access to OneDrive, specifically the **CAD Files** folder and the **Client Files** folder. (Video 6, 0:32)

## Key Terms

1. **Buffer** means the clipboard. When the video says "copy it into your buffer," it means Ctrl+C so the value is ready to paste.
2. **Artifacts folder** means the project's folder in Smartsheet that holds all of its sheets, reports, subfolders, and the dashboard.
3. **Gen 009** is the name of the project checklist template.
4. **Account number** means the project number, copied with no extra spaces and no dash, just the digits. (Video 2, 1:03)

## Process at a Glance

1. Receive the alert email.
2. Create the project checklist from the Gen 009 template.
3. Set up the new checklist sheet (name, number, columns, filter).
4. Share the checklist with Ashley.
5. Verify the signed letter of agreement in the Master Project List.
6. Update the filter and name on all five "Update Project Filter" reports.
7. Trim the project plan down to one section.
8. Publish the Project Orders report and embed it in the dashboard.
9. Create a project-specific filter in the Orders sheet.
10. Create the Dynamic View (admin profile) and set its filter and sharing.
11. Link the Dynamic View to the dashboard.
12. Create the two OneDrive folders.
13. Close out and archive once the team confirms.

## Task 1: Create the Project Checklist from the Gen 009 Template (Video 1, 0:23 to 1:14)

1. Open the checklist template. It is called **Gen 009** (shown on screen as "New Gen 009").
2. In the template, click **File**.
3. Click **Save as**, then **Save as New**. This creates a copy so nothing you do can mess up the template.
4. Name the new sheet: the **project name, followed by the words "Gen 009"**.
   1. Example format: `1815 Lakewood Road Gen 009`
5. Wait for the save to complete. You are now working inside the new sheet, not the template. (Video 1, 1:14)

**How you know it worked:** The sheet title at the top shows the project name plus "Gen 009," and edits you make do not appear in the template.

## Task 3: Share the Checklist with Ashley (Video 1, 2:07 to 2:51)

The checklist row reads "Grant Ashley write access to the sheet and send Ashley the link." Both happen in one action:

1. In the checklist sheet, go to the **Share** tab.
2. In the invite field, **type Ashley's name manually**.
   1. Warning from the video: she will not always appear in the suggestions, so do not rely on autofill. Type the name out.
3. Make sure you select the **correct Ashley**. (In the video there were two entries that happened to be the same person, but check every time.)
4. Set the permission level to **Editor, Can Share**.
5. Leave **"Notify people"** checked. This is what sends her the link, so you do not need to send a separate message.
6. Click **Share**. The sheet is now shared and the link has been emailed to her.
7. Back in the checklist, mark this row as **Done**.
8. Click **Save**. (Video 1, 2:41)

**How you know it worked:** Ashley appears in the sheet's collaborator list as Editor, Can Share.

## Task 5: Update the Filter and Name on All Five Project Reports (Video 2, 0:54 to 5:00, and Video 3, 0:00 to 0:12)

This is the most repetitive part of the process. The speaker says on camera: "If there's one thing that I want automated, more than anything else, it's this." (Video 2, 0:57)

### 5A. Copy the account number

1. In the checklist sheet, select the **account number** you typed in Task 2.
2. Copy it. Rules for the copy: **no extra spaces, no dash, just the number**. (Video 2, 1:03)
3. Keep it in your clipboard. You will paste it ten times in this task (five filters, five report names).

### 5B. Navigate to the project's artifacts folder

1. Go to the **Project List**.
2. Next to the project's name is the **Project Dashboard link**. Click it. (Video 2, 1:26)
3. In the dashboard, click **File**, then **Properties**.
4. In the properties window, click the **final step of the folder path** (the last folder shown in the breadcrumb).
5. You are now inside the **project's artifacts folder**. It contains subfolders plus the project's sheets, reports, and dashboard. (Video 2, 1:47)

### 5C. Update each of the five reports

There are **five reports** whose names contain the words "update project" (the exact wording varies, for example "Update Project Filter"). In the video, **two** sit at the folder level you land in, and **three more** are inside a subfolder. (Video 2, 1:57 to 2:20)

For **each** of the five reports, do exactly this:

1. Click into the report.
2. Open the **Filter**.
3. Find the **project number filter**. It will contain a long number left over from the template.
4. Select whatever is there and **replace it by pasting** the account number from your clipboard. (Video 2, 2:30)
5. Wait a moment. It takes time to apply, and the video warns "all the loading and waiting can drive the person crazy." Do not click ahead while it loads. (Video 2, 3:05)
6. Change the **report header (its name)** to the account number. This is deliberate: the renamed header is your **visual confirmation** that the report is done. (Video 2, 2:48)
7. Single-click to back out of the report.
8. Move to the next report and repeat steps 1 through 7.
9. When you finish the reports at the first level, go into the **subfolder** and do the remaining ones the same way. When the subfolder is done, go **back up one level** to the folder you came from and finish any left there. (Video 2, 4:03)

### 5D. Mark it done

1. When all five reports show the account number as their header, go back to the checklist.
2. Mark **"Update filters in all project reports"** as **Done**. (Video 3, 0:06)

**Warnings and notes for this task:**

1. **Do not rely on report names containing the full project name.** When the system spun up the project, it may have truncated the name, so the artifact names are inconsistent. Identify the reports by the "update project" wording, not by the project name. (Video 2, 3:43)
2. Planned improvement stated on camera: rename the report in the spin-up template so the wording is **identical in all five**, wrapped in **double curly braces** before and after, so future automation can reliably find what needs updating. (Video 2, 4:17 to 4:34)

**How you know it worked:** All five reports display the account number as their header, and each one's filter contains the account number.

## Task 6: Trim the Project Plan to One Section (Video 3, 0:12 to 1:11)

1. From the artifacts area, back up one level to the **project artifacts folder**.
2. Open the artifact called **CMR Project Plan** (the project plan that comes built into every project).
3. The plan contains **three sections**. Every project needs only **one**, based on the project type.
4. Delete the two sections that do not apply.
   1. Example from the video: the project was **commercial**, so the **Residential** section and the **Patterson** section were deleted.
5. Warning from the video: **nothing on screen tells you the project type.** You have to know what kind of project it is before deleting. If you are not sure, confirm the type before touching the plan. (Video 3, 0:46)
6. Click **Save**.
7. Go back to the checklist and mark this row as **Done**.

**How you know it worked:** The project plan contains exactly one section, and it matches the project type.

## Task 7: Publish the Project Orders Report and Embed It in the Dashboard (Video 4)

This task touches two artifacts: the **Project Orders report** and the **Project Dashboard**.

### 7A. Publish the report and copy the embed code

1. From any artifact of the project, click **File**, then **Properties**, then click the folder in the path. (The video calls this "just the easiest way to navigate to the folder containing all the artifacts.") (Video 4, 0:11)
2. In the artifacts folder, find the report called **Project Orders** and open it.
3. Click the **Publish** icon.
4. **Turn publishing on**. (Video 4, 0:57)
5. In the publish panel, find the section called **Embed code**.
6. A **single click** in that field is enough to highlight all of it. Copy it. (Video 4, 1:04)
7. **No save is needed.** Close the report. The embed code is in your clipboard.
8. Important distinction from the video: what you copied is the **published link** for the report, which is **different from the report's URL**. It looks more like an iframe link. Do not substitute the address-bar URL. (Video 4, 1:19)

### 7B. Paste it into the dashboard

1. Go back to the project artifacts folder (File, Properties, click the folder).
2. Open the **Project Dashboard**.
3. Click **Edit** to enter edit mode.
4. Scroll down, almost all the way to the bottom.
5. Find the section **right below the pencil** that says **"Project Orders update filters view only."** (Video 4, 1:57)
6. **Double-click** it. A panel appears that says **Web Content**.
7. Click the Web Content field **once**.
8. **Highlight everything** that is currently in the field.
9. **Paste** the embed code from your clipboard. It will look like an iframe link once pasted. (Video 4, 2:19)
10. Click **Apply**.
11. Watch the widget change. Before, it said "read only" and did not show the project number. After a correct paste, it shows the **project number**. The video's exact words: "You know you got it right." (Video 4, 2:26)
12. Click **Save**.
13. Go back to the checklist and mark this row as **Done**.

**How you know it worked:** The dashboard widget now displays the project number instead of "read only."

## Task 8: Create the Project-Specific Filter in the Orders Sheet (Video 5, 0:00 to 1:28)

**Read the timing warning at the end of this task before starting the next one. Order matters here.**

1. In the checklist, copy the **account number** again so it is in your clipboard. (The video keeps it in the checklist exactly because "we're going to be referencing it a lot.")
2. Click the checklist's **link to the Orders sheet**. Give it a moment to load.
3. In the Orders sheet, click the **small drop-down arrow on the left** (the filter menu). (Video 5, 0:29)
4. **Scroll to the bottom** of the filter list.
5. Find one of the **existing filters** that has the icons on the side and **just a number as its name**. These are the previous projects' filters.
6. **Copy** that filter (duplicate it). You are using it as your starting point.
7. In the copy:
   1. Paste the account number as the **filter's name**.
   2. Paste the account number as the **filter's criteria value**.
   3. In the video's words: "you're giving the filter the name of that account number, and you're actually filtering it by that number." (Video 5, 0:49)
8. Click **Apply**.
9. Click **Save**.
10. Go back to the checklist and mark this row as **Done**.

**Critical timing warning (Video 5, 1:10 to 1:28):**

1. This filter must exist **before** you open Dynamic View in the next task.
2. The filter takes **several seconds to propagate** to the other forms and views that use it.
3. If the Dynamic View pages were already open in your browser **before** you saved this filter, they **will not see it**. You would have to reload them. Create and save the filter first, then open Dynamic View.

**How you know it worked:** The Orders sheet's filter list now contains a filter named with your account number, and later, that filter appears as an option inside Dynamic View.

## Task 9: Create the Dynamic View (Video 5, 1:28 to 5:23)

### 9A. Switch to the admin browser profile

The next two tasks require **different credentials**.

1. Click your **browser profile icon** (top corner of the browser).
2. Open the profile called **Smartsheet Admin**. (Video 5, 1:42)
3. Why a separate profile: if you log into Dynamic View in your normal profile, it conflicts with your regular session. Your other windows would get logged out of your user, or the login would open in the wrong session. The separate profile keeps the two logins isolated. (Video 5, 2:16 to 2:23)
4. In the admin profile, go to **Dynamic View**.
5. Sign in with **email and password**. The password is saved in that profile's browser cache. (Video 5, 2:02 to 2:09)

### 9B. Create the new view by copying the most recent one

The checklist instructions read: "Create a dynamic view: open Dynamic View, select the most recent dynamic view, click the gear, and do Save as New. Name it with the project name, project number, and the word orders." (Video 5, 2:34 to 2:42)

1. Before you start, copy the **project name and number** from the checklist into your clipboard. You will need it for the rename. (Video 5, 2:49)
2. In Dynamic View, select the **most recent** dynamic view. (You can sanity-check that you picked the right kind: old projects all follow the same naming format.) (Video 5, 3:14)
3. Click the **gear** icon.
4. Click **Save as New**. You now have a copy that needs renaming.
5. Click the **gear** again, go to **View Name**.
6. Paste the project name and number from your clipboard, then **add the word "orders"** to the end. (Video 5, 3:24 to 3:30)
   1. Format: `[Project Name] [Project Number] orders`
7. Click **Save** now, so you do not lose the work. (Video 5, 3:37)

### 9C. Set the filter

1. Still in the view's settings, go down to the **Filter** setting.
2. Select the filter you created in Task 8. In the video the filter **110216** appears in the list. (Video 5, 4:10)
3. The video's proof of the timing rule: "if we hadn't created the other sheet, it wouldn't be here." If your filter is missing, it either was not saved or has not propagated yet. Wait, reload, and check again.
4. Click **Save**. (Video 5, 4:20)

### 9D. Share the view

1. Go to the **Sharing** tab of the dynamic view. (Video 5, 4:37)
2. In **Share domains**, enter: **cmr-design.com** (Video 5, 4:41)
3. Confirm the entry with the add or confirm button next to the field. [The transcript garbles this moment ("I think we'll be putting the right button"), so verify the exact button against the video.]
4. Click **Save**. (Video 5, 5:10)

**How you know it worked:** The new view exists, named in the standard format ending in "orders," its filter is your account number filter, and cmr-design.com is listed under shared domains.

## Task 12: Close Out and Archive (Video 6, 2:06 to end)

1. The final checklist row cannot be closed immediately. It waits on the **other team members**.
2. Keep your checklist filtered to your own items. When the other team members notify you that they are done with everything else, switch to the view that shows **all done / any items**. (Video 6, 2:20 to 2:33)

4. **Archive** the checklist. The project spin-up is finished. (Video 6, 2:37)
